A stock jumps. The chart tells you when. The harder question is what else moved with it.

LimitUp brings watchlists, price-and-volume charts, market news and A-share market review into one workspace. Start with a company, widen out to its sector, and come back to the chart with more context.

No local setup is needed to try the web app. **[Start at big-a88.com](https://big-a88.com/)**.

## What you can do

| Your question | Where to start |
|---|---|
| **What's moving on my list?** | Track stocks across China A-shares, Hong Kong and the U.S., with watchlists and intraday views. |
| **Does the move hold up across timeframes?** | Read candlestick charts, volume and technical indicators. The product offers up to 15 timeframes and 52 chart controls across its plans. |
| **Is this a stock story or a market story?** | Compare major and global indices, market news, and stock and sector capital-flow views. |
| **What happened in the A-share session?** | Review limit-up sequences, Dragon-Tiger List disclosures (*Longhu Bang*), research reports and capital flows. |
| **Can I keep the same workspace on desktop?** | Use the web app or get the desktop edition from the website, with account-linked watchlists and preferences. |

## Built around a research routine

**Start with your watchlist.** Pick the companies you actually follow rather than chasing every flashing number.

**Check price and volume together.** Move between intraday and longer-term charts. Use indicators to test an idea, not to manufacture certainty.

**Read the surrounding market.** Look at the index, sector and relevant news before treating an individual move as a signal.

**Review the session.** For A-shares, follow the limit-up ladder and trading disclosures, then decide what deserves a closer look tomorrow.

LimitUp is for people who want a clearer research process—not a promise that the next trade will win.

## A few things worth knowing

- **China-market depth, with a broader watchlist.** A-share review tools sit alongside Hong Kong and U.S. stock coverage. Features and data coverage are not identical across markets.
- **Your choice of interface.** English and Chinese, light and dark themes, web and desktop.
- **Start small.** Basic, Pro, Max and Ultra have different feature access. Check the website for current plans, trial conditions and desktop downloads.
- **Check the data before acting.** Availability and refresh rates depend on the market and data source. Research tools, indicators and historical results are not investment advice or a guarantee of future performance.

# 涨停吧 · LimitUp

**不只看见涨跌，也看清涨跌背后的线索。**

一只股票突然拉升，K 线能告诉你它什么时候动了。更值得追问的是：成交量有没有跟上？板块是否在动？消息、资金和盘后披露能不能相互印证？

涨停吧把自选、分时 K 线、行情快讯和 A 股复盘放在同一个工作台。先看公司，再看板块和市场，最后回到图上做判断，不必在几个页面之间反复拼线索。

### 从你关心的问题开始

| 你想知道什么 | 可以从哪里看 |
|---|---|
| **自选今天有什么变化？** | A 股、港股、美股自选与分时走势，集中查看自己真正关注的标的。 |

| **今天的 A 股该怎么复盘？** | 涨停梯队、龙虎榜、研报与资金流，整理值得继续跟踪的线索。 |

### 一套顺手的看盘、复盘流程

**先看自选。** 从熟悉的公司出发，不被每一次跳动牵着走。

**再看量价。** 分时和长周期一起看，让指标帮助验证判断，而不是替你作决定。

**把市场背景补齐。** 对照指数、板块与消息，区分个股变化和整体行情。

**收盘后整理。** 回看涨停梯队与交易披露，把明天值得研究的问题留下来。
